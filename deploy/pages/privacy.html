The current website

Simple pages. Limited data needs.

This notice covers the public pages, account area, and contact form on the Cellar Studio website. Separate applications, integrations and linked providers may have different data practices.

Accounts & payments

Optional accounts.

You can browse as a guest. Where account access is enabled, Cellar Studio offers email and Google sign-in. Cellar Studio does not process payments on this site.

Browser preferences

Your choice stays in-browser.

Local storage remembers the theme mode (cellar-studio-theme), custom palette (cellar-studio-custom-theme), saved themes (cellar-studio-custom-library), and privacy preferences (cellar-privacy-preferences). Clear site storage to reset them.

Infrastructure

Hosting & fonts.

Vercel serves the site and may process standard request information. Google Fonts can receive requests when fonts load. Their terms govern those services.

Data inventory

What you enter. What we receive.

When you select Send message, your browser submits the form to FormSubmit. It forwards the message to Cellar Studio at cellarcsv@gmail.com. We use this information to respond to your enquiry.

Account information
Supabase Auth stores your name, email address, sign-in methods, last sign-in time, and account identifiers to manage your account. Your account also stores any organization, job title, and website you choose to save. If you set a password, Supabase Auth stores a password hash. When you use Google, the provider authenticates you and shares the information needed for sign-in. If Google provides a profile photo URL, Supabase Auth can store it with your account. Your browser loads the photo from Google for the account menu and profile page. Cellar Studio does not receive the password for those provider accounts.
Account session
Essential cookies keep you signed in. If you choose Keep me signed in, the browser can retain your session for up to 30 days, subject to session validity. Sign out to clear it from your device.
Theme & privacy choices
Stored in your browser's local storage
Optional name and reply email
Submitted and included in the email when provided
Enquiry topic and message
Submitted and included in the email
Form consent
Checked in the browser before submission
Sent enquiries

Website assistant questions
When you ask a question, the current page name, your question, and recent chat messages may be sent through Vercel AI Gateway to the model provider with relevant public website text. New chat clears the conversation from this page. The assistant does not attach your CSV, contract, or form fields. Leave private information out of questions.
CSV checker files and pasted text
Processed in your browser and kept on your device
Saved Cellar Guard rules
If you choose to remember your rules, the required column names and delimiter are saved on this device. Use Forget saved rules in the checker to remove them.
Site analytics or advertising cookies
Not installed by this site's source code

FormSubmit provides spam screening and retains submissions for 30 days. Gmail processes the forwarded messages. FormSubmit data handling

Boundaries matter

No hidden promises about data.

Links leave our site.Third-party destinations, hosting and fonts have their own data practices. Optional iframe embeds stay off until explicitly enabled, but the setting does not block fonts or hosting requests.

Features can change.Contact us to ask about your account data or request deletion.

No certification claim.This page describes implemented behavior; it is not an independent privacy audit or a legal-compliance certification.
